Present Simple

Put the verb in brackets into the correct form.

Positive Negative Question
The rule Present Simple
Positive Negative Question
I / you / we / they I work in a bank. I don't work in a bank. Do you work in a bank?
he / she / it She works in a bank. She doesn't work in a bank. Does she work in a bank?
  • We use the Present Simple for habits, routines and facts: She gets up at seven. The sun rises in the east. Present Simple используется для привычек, распорядка дня и фактов.
  • With he / she / it the verb takes -s: I work → she works. С he / she / it к глаголу добавляется -s.
  • In negatives and questions, do / does shows the person, and the main verb stays in its base form: She doesn't work (not doesn't works), Does she work? (not Does she works?). В отрицаниях и вопросах лицо показывает do / does, а основной глагол остаётся в базовой форме.
He / she / it: spelling
Most verbs: + s work → works  ·  play → plays  ·  live → lives
-ch, -sh, -s, -x, -o: + es watch → watches  ·  wash → washes  ·  go → goes  ·  do → does
consonant + y: y → ies study → studies  ·  try → tries
Irregular have → has
In the exercises: in negatives write the whole form (don't work or does not work). In questions fill in both gaps: do / does and the verb. Capital letters don't matter.
