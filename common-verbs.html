Common verbs

Complete the sentences with am, is, are, have or has. In questions and negatives with have, use do / does or don't / doesn't as well. Some questions have two boxes: one for the auxiliary (do/does) and one for the verb (have). Capital letters don't matter, and for negatives the short and full forms are both fine (e.g. isn't or is not).
